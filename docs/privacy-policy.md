# Vechelon — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 06/23/2026
**Provided by:** Product Delivered Inc. ("Vechelon", "we", "us")
**Contact:** neil@productdelivered.ca

## 1\. Who we are

Vechelon is a platform that cycling **clubs** use to run group rides. Clubs are our customers; their riders use the Vechelon mobile app and web portal. Each club is a separate tenant — **clubs cannot see each other's riders or data.**

## 2\. What we collect

* **Account information:** your email address, display name, and (optionally) phone number.
* **Club membership:** which club(s) you belong to and your role on a ride (rider, captain, support).
* **Ride participation:** the rides you join and basic participation records.
* **Real-time location:** while you are in an **active ride**, your device's GPS position — including when the app is in the background or your screen is locked (see §4).

## 3\. How we use your information

* To show your **live position to your own club** during an active ride (fleet awareness, rider safety, support coordination).
* To manage your account, club membership, and ride participation.
* To send you sign-in (magic-link / one-time-code) and ride-related emails.
* We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

## 4\. Location data — how we handle it (important)

* **We transmit your position in real time; we do not build a location history.** During an active ride your position is broadcast live to your club so members can see the group on the map. **These positions are not stored as a durable track or trip history on our servers** — once a ride ends they are not retained as a location record.
* **Background location** is used **only during an active ride**, so your club can still see you when your screen is off or the app is backgrounded — which is the core safety purpose of a live group ride. We do not track your location when you are not in an active ride.
* You control location access through your device's permission settings and can revoke it at any time (this will disable live tracking during rides).
* Location is collected on-device by the Transistorsoft background-geolocation engine and transmitted over our realtime channel; it is not shared with third parties for their own purposes.

## 5\. Who can see your data

* **Within your club only.** Per the ride role model, your club's **captain and support** can see all riders' live positions and contact details during a ride; other riders see positions per the club's visibility rules.
* **Tenant isolation:** riders and staff of other clubs cannot see your data.
* We never share your personal data with third parties except the service providers in §6 who process it on our behalf.

## 6\. Service providers

We use these processors strictly to operate the service:

* **Supabase** — application backend, database, authentication.
* **Transistorsoft** — on-device background-geolocation engine.
* **Google Maps** — map display.
* **Resend** — transactional email (sign-in and ride emails).

## 7\. Data retention

* **Account and club data:** retained while your account is active.
* **Location/position data:** **not retained** as a location history (see §4).
* **Ride records:** basic ride/participation records are retained for your club's operational needs.
* You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data (see §8).

## 8\. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including PIPEDA in Canada), you may request to **access, correct, or delete** your personal information, and withdraw consent for optional processing. Contact `neil@productdelivered.ca`. We will respond within the period required by law.

## 9\. Children

Vechelon is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 10\. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, access controls, tenant isolation enforced at the database level) to protect your data. No method is perfectly secure, but the fact that we **do not store a location history** materially limits what could ever be exposed.

## 11\. Changes

We may update this policy; we will post the new effective date here and, for material changes, notify you in-app or by email.
