Delete your account

App: Vechelon (Android) Provided by: Product Delivered Inc. Contact: neil@productdelivered.ca

You can delete your Vechelon account and its data at any time, with or without the app. Deleting is permanent and can’t be undone.

1.In the app

  • Open Vechelon and sign in.
  • On the home screen, tap Delete account (next to Privacy Policy).
  • Read what is deleted, then type DELETE to confirm. If you’re on a ride, leave the ride first.

2.On the web, without the app

  • Go to your club’s Vechelon site — the address in your ride invitations, ending in .vechelon.ca — and sign in with your email.
  • Open Profile (or go straight to your club’s address followed by /profile) and choose Delete account at the bottom.
  • Read what is deleted, then type DELETE to confirm. You’re signed out and your account is gone.

3.By email

If you can’t sign in, email neil@productdelivered.ca from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Vechelon account”. We’ll reply to confirm, then delete it the same way.

4.What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, profile, photo and club memberships.
  • Your name, email, phone and last position on rides you joined with this account or this email address. Those rides show "Deleted rider" instead.
  • Your beacon alerts, ride status and any phone invite you joined from.

Vechelon never stores your ride positions as a location history, so there is no track to delete. See the Privacy Policy.

5.What stays

  • Rides and routes you created, and rides you were assigned to support, stay with your club, credited to "Former member".
  • Guest sign-ups made under a different email address, and never linked to this account, stay as they are.
  • Anonymous usage statistics stay, but can no longer be linked to you.
  • Our sign-in provider’s logs may keep your email address for about a day.

6.Club organisers

If you’re the only organiser of a club, make another member an organiser first, then delete your account. That keeps the club running for its riders.
