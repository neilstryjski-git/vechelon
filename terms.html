1.What Vechelon is

Vechelon helps cycling clubs run group rides. During an active ride, the club’s Captain and support crew can see riders on a live map, riders can see their Captain and support crew, and a rider can raise a Beacon to ask for help. Clubs also use the web portal to manage members and schedule rides.

Each club is a separate space. Clubs are our customers; you use Vechelon as a member of a club.
